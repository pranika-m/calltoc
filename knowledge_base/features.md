# ApexCRM — Product Features

## Visual Pipeline & Lead Management
Track every prospect from first enquiry to closed deal using customizable Kanban stages and table views. Filter leads by score, sentiment, owner, or expected close date.

## Automated Lead Scoring
Score leads automatically on a 0–100 scale based on requirement clarity, budget confirmation, purchase timeline, decision-maker status, and interest level.

## Smart Appointment Scheduling
Share real-time calendar availability and book demos without double-booking. Automatic reminders reduce no-shows.

## Team Analytics & Forecasting
Monitor total calls, qualified lead rates, booking conversion rates, top objections, and pipeline value in real time.

## Integrations
Native integrations with Google Calendar, Gmail, Outlook, Slack, and REST webhooks for custom workflows.
