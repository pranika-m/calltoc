# ApexCRM — Pricing Plans

All plans are billed monthly or annually (save 20% with annual billing).

## Starter Plan — $29 per user / month
- Up to 5 team members
- 2,500 active contacts
- Visual sales pipeline and task reminders
- Email integration and CSV import

## Growth Plan — $59 per user / month (Most Popular)
- Up to 50 team members
- 25,000 active contacts
- AI lead scoring and call summaries
- Automated follow-up sequences and calendar booking
- Priority chat and phone support

## Enterprise Plan — Custom Pricing
- Unlimited team members and contacts
- Dedicated account manager and custom SLA
- Role-based access control and audit logs
- Custom API and webhook integrations

Note: On-premise self-hosted deployment is only available on custom Enterprise contracts; we do not publish a standard self-hosted price.
