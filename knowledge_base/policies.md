# ApexCRM — Policies & Security

## Data Security & Privacy
All customer data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Each workspace is strictly isolated by tenant ID.

## Cancellation & Refunds
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time with no cancellation fee; access continues until the end of the current billing cycle. Annual plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee from the purchase date.

## Uptime SLA
Growth and Enterprise plans are backed by a 99.9% uptime SLA.
