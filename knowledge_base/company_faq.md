# ApexCRM — Company FAQ

## What is ApexCRM?
ApexCRM is an AI-assisted customer relationship management platform built for fast-moving sales teams, including real estate agencies, B2B SaaS, education, and financial services.

## How long does onboarding take?
Standard onboarding takes 24 hours. Our team imports your existing CSV or spreadsheet leads and configures your pipeline stages during a 30-minute kickoff call.

## Do you offer a free trial?
Yes. Every new workspace includes a 14-day free trial with full access to pipeline management, automated reminders, and team analytics. No credit card is required to start.

## What support channels are included?
All plans include email and live chat support during business hours (Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM). Growth and Enterprise plans include priority Slack/phone support and a dedicated account manager.

## Can we migrate from spreadsheets or HubSpot?
Yes. ApexCRM supports one-click CSV import and guided migration from HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho CRM with zero data loss.
